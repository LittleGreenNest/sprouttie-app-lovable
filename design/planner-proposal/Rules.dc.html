Five things to settle before this gets built.

Four have a recommendation. The fifth is the same blocker as the Home proposal and should be decided once, there, for both screens.

1 How many words a week?

The app offers 1, 2, 3 or 5 sets, roughly 5 to 25 words. The brand promise is five.

Recommendation

Five a week, always. But the composition adapts: if three did not land last week, this week is three carried forward plus two new. Momentum shows up in the mix, not the number. Drop the set picker.

2 Does confirming lock the week?

Once you tap Start this week, can a word still be swapped on Wednesday?

Recommendation

Yes, swapping stays open all week. Locking would make a bad Monday suggestion a bad seven days.

3 What if last week was never reflected on?

Monday arrives, the parent skipped Sunday. Block, nag, or move on?

Recommendation

Move on. Carry the unrated words forward silently and ask again next Sunday. Blocking the week on a missed reflection punishes the week someone was already too busy for.

4 What happens to the two entry points?

The dashboard “This Week” card and /word-planner both exist today.

Recommendation

One screen, reached from the dashboard card. /word-planner redirects to it. No second planner.

5 What earns “Growing”?

Still unsettled from the Home proposal, and it blocks this too: the Sunday review is where stages get set, so the rule decides what the review writes.

Still open

Not recommended here. This is the same open decision as board 4 of the Home canvas. Settle it there, once, for both.

Recommendations, not decisions. Nothing here is built.
